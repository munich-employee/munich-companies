## Allianz Technology SE - The Engineering Entity

Allianz Technology SE is Allianz Group's global IT service provider, headquartered in Munich. It is the principal driver behind Allianz's digital transformation - tasked to run, optimize, transform, and innovate infrastructure, applications, and services.

- **11,000+ employees** across 20+ countries
- Ranked **2nd globally** in the Evident AI Insurance Index 2025 - one of only two insurers scoring top five in every AI category
- Great Place to Work certified in **15 countries** (2025)
- EDGE Certification for workplace inclusion across 10 locations (80%+ of workforce)
- ~80% of positions advertised require some level of AI knowledge

## Location

**Allianz Technology SE HQ:** Königinstraße 28, 80802 München (Schwabing, near Englischer Garten)

**Allianz Technology GmbH branch:** Fritz-Schäffer-Str. 9, 81737 München (Neuperlach)

**Allianz Deutschland / AI & Data teams: Dieselstr. 8, 85774 Unterföhring** - this is the most likely office for AI/ML roles. Job postings for AI & Cloud Principal Engineer and Data & AI Innovation are located here.

**Work model:** Hybrid - Allianz Germany guideline is **4 days presence in the office per month**. Average mobile working rate in German entities is **62%**. In practice, most employees are in the office 2-3 days per week, some less. 90% of employees worldwide report satisfaction with the flexible working model. Employees can also work up to 25 days per year abroad.

This is one of the most generous hybrid policies among large German corporates.

### Review Themes (2025-2026)

**Positives:**
- Strong work-life balance - genuinely respected, especially in the Technology arm
- Generous benefits package (30 days PTO, flexible hybrid, wellness programs)
- Good salary relative to insurance industry standards
- Collaborative culture within tech teams
- Investment in training and development
- Modern tools and best practices in engineering teams
- Diversity and inclusion rated highly (4.4 by SWEs)

**Negatives:**
- **Corporate bureaucracy** - slow decision-making, heavy processes (typical for 156K-person organization)
- **Career progression is slow** - depends on project, manager, and tenure; seniority/politics can matter more than merit
- **Salary below tech industry standards** - fair for insurance, below market for pure tech
- **Internal consulting arm (ECON) is toxic** - reports of dishonesty, hierarchy, ego-driven management. This is a separate division from Technology
- **Some Technology-specific complaints:** outdated infrastructure in some teams, communication gaps across departments

### Toxic Leadership Assessment

No systemic toxic leadership signal in Allianz Technology. The worst reviews come from the internal consulting division (ECON) and energy/operations divisions outside Germany. The Technology arm scores significantly higher (4.2 SWE vs 3.9 company average). The risk is **corporate bureaucracy and slow pace**, not toxicity. Comparable to past experience.

## Regulated Industry - CAUTION

Insurance is heavily regulated (Solvency II, BaFin oversight, GDPR, EU AI Act). Practical implications:
- More compliance overhead in AI/ML work (model governance, explainability requirements)
- Slower deployment cycles compared to pure tech companies
- However, Allianz Technology was specifically structured to operate more like a tech company within the group
- The EU AI Act implications actually create demand for AI governance/engineering talent

## On-Call - CAUTION

Some Allianz Technology roles explicitly mention on-call duty:
- Software Architect: "willingness to participate in on-call duty and weekend assignments"
- Tech Lead: "on-call duty support out of office hours...to be provided in rotations"

AI/ML engineering roles do not mention on-call in their descriptions, but this should be verified early in any conversation - particularly for platform engineering roles that may involve production infrastructure.

## Verdict

Allianz is an exceptionally stable employer with a genuine, well-resourced technology arm (11,000+ engineers), a Python-centric AI/ML stack, and one of the most generous hybrid work policies among large German corporates. The AI/data roles are likely based in Unterföhring, with only 4 days/month in-office. SWE-specific reviews are strong (4.2, 89% recommend, 4.4 WLB).

The two blockers are:

1. **Compensation** - insurance pay bands are structurally below tech industry standards. The target is a stretch even for senior AI/ML roles, likely requiring top-of-band negotiation. Standard SWE comp (~€77K median) is far below target.

2. **Corporate bureaucracy** - this is a 156,000-person insurance conglomerate. Decision-making is slow, career progression depends on politics/tenure, and processes are heavy. If past corporate experience were manageable, Allianz Technology would feel similar.
