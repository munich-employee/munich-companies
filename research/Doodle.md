# Doodle - Full Research Report

_Researched: 2026-11-13_

> **Critical Disqualifiers**
> 1. **No Munich office.** Doodle's offices are Zurich (HQ), Berlin, Belgrade and Atlanta. Senior software engineering roles are advertised as **Berlin, Hybrid**, and every posting states that Doodle "cannot sponsor visas or support relocation". Only some data roles (Data Infrastructure Engineer, Analytics Engineer) are advertised as **Fully Remote, Germany**.
> 2. **Pay below the Munich large-employer bracket.** Glassdoor Berlin shows Senior Software Engineer at €70-89K and Senior Frontend Engineer at €72-85K, with no equity. levels.fyi has no Doodle profile.
> 3. **Profitability not demonstrated.** The parent (TX Group) describes revenue as "in the double-digit million range" (CHF) and says the subscription transition "established a solid foundation for profitable growth". 2025 revenue declined slightly; EBITDA "increased significantly" but no figure or sign is disclosed.
>
> Full report follows for completeness.

## Section 6: AI/ML & Tech Stack

### AI Strategy

- **Product:** The 2026 relaunch is positioned as **Doodle Time OS** ("the new operating system of time"): a layer above calendars that reads calendars automatically, enforces meeting-density limits ("Protect"), protects focus time, and coordinates "humans and AI agents". Related new features: My Time (day view), Collaboration Rooms, Round Robin, a reimagined Group Poll and Booking Page. Time OS is still "coming soon" / early access in November 2026.
- **Thought leadership:** The "Doodle Time Institute" and the CEO's framing that "scheduling is solved" and the value now lies in an agent-coordination layer.
- **Engineering practice:** Every 2026 engineering posting makes LLM/agentic development central: "We actively encourage our engineers to use LLM and agentic tools (Specs, Loops, and agentic workflows)... We stay provider agnostic (Anthropic, OpenAI, Cursor, local models, or self hosted models in the cloud)."
- **ML roles:** Recent, now-closed postings "Full Stack AI Engineer: Doodle AI Ecosystem" (URL slug "machine-learning-engineer-ai-systems") and "Data Infrastructure & MLOps Engineer" show some applied ML/LLM work. A candidate review of an AI Engineer take-home (Oct 2025) mentions "Build a classical ML and a deep L models and compare their tradeoffs".
- **Assessment:** Applied LLM features inside a scheduling product, plus heavy use of AI coding tools. No evidence of an ML platform, model training at scale or MLOps infrastructure beyond one data-infrastructure role. Today the open software roles are product engineering (Java backend, growth fullstack), not AI/ML.

## Section 13: Verdict

**Exclude for the advertised software engineering roles; monitor only for a role explicitly advertised as fully remote in Germany.**

**Strengths:** Doodle is a genuine product company with a well-known brand (~30M monthly users), a direct German employing entity whose registered purpose is software development, English as the working language, no regulatory overhead, and a modern, event-driven Java/Go/Kafka stack. AI is genuinely part of how it builds software. It has historically offered good work-life balance, flexible hours and solid benefits (education and wellbeing budgets), holds a GPTW Switzerland certification, and the backing of a listed parent removes VC runway risk.

**Hard blockers:**
1. **No Munich office and hybrid Berlin roles.** Both open senior software engineering roles are "Berlin, Hybrid", and Doodle states it cannot support relocation. Only data-infrastructure and analytics roles are currently fully remote in Germany.
2. **Compensation below bracket.** Glassdoor Berlin shows senior software engineers at €70-89K (3 data points) and senior frontend at €72-85K, with no equity and no bonus, against ~€85-110K base at large, profitable Munich employers. levels.fyi has no data to suggest otherwise.
3. **Profitability not demonstrated.** Doodle has spent a decade moving from ad funding to subscriptions; its own parent framed the result as a "foundation for profitable growth", and 2025 revenue fell slightly. No profit figure is published.

**Secondary concerns:**
- Repeated leadership and strategy changes (2019, 2022-2023, 2025), at least two unannounced layoff rounds (2023-2024), and a 2025-2026 restructuring that a long-tenured Belgrade engineer said "took a turn for worse".
- No works council or collective agreement found at the Berlin GmbH, plus a 2025 review saying staff who disagree are "let go".
- Kununu 3.2 with 50% recommendation diverges sharply from Glassdoor's 4.4, and recent Glassdoor positives come mostly from staff with less than a year's tenure.
- Company-wide bet on an unreleased product (Time OS) in a crowded market; TX Group could divest a non-core holding if it fails.
- Take-home-heavy hiring process with a 35% positive candidate experience.
- Likely incident duty for backend/data roles at a ~100-person, 24/7 SaaS.

**Recommendation:** Do not pursue the current Berlin-hybrid engineering roles. If Doodle advertises a senior/staff engineering role as fully remote within Germany, it is worth a look only if the recruiter confirms upfront (1) a salary band at or above ~€90K base, (2) that the role is permanent fully remote with no expectation of regular Berlin presence, (3) the on-call model, and (4) how the 2025-2026 restructuring affected engineering headcount.
