# ElevenLabs - Full Research Report

_Researched: 2026-10-05_

> **Critical Disqualifiers**
> 1. **No German legal entity.** NorthData has no ElevenLabs company registered in Germany (the only "Eleven Labs GmbH", HRB 58099 Düsseldorf, is an unrelated web company). German staff are therefore employed by a foreign group company or through an Employer of Record. A direct German employment contract is **UNVERIFIED** and unlikely.
> 2. **Not a mature, proven-profitable business.** Founded 2022, venture-backed ($781M primary funding), valued at $22B on ~$600M ARR. Group profitability is not disclosed. The only "profitable" claim is a secondary source about late 2024.
> 3. **Work intensity and firing culture.** Glassdoor reviews from engineers and non-engineers alike report evenings and weekends, "60+ hours is the norm" (FDE), dismissals before the end of probation, and "when in doubt, fire the person". In Germany there is no works council and no local entity to absorb this.

Research Engineering covers training, post-training, evals, inference optimisation (CUDA, Triton, TensorRT, vLLM, SGLang) and data infrastructure (crawlers, curation, classifiers). It runs its own NVIDIA GPU clusters (bare metal plus rented capacity, Slurm, InfiniBand/RoCE). The product layer (ElevenAgents) is an enterprise agent platform with integrations, testing and monitoring, and uses Gemini as a reasoning LLM option. Google Cloud partnership press releases (Feb and May 2026) and a Google Cloud Applied AI Partner of the Year award. Internally the company is "AI first", with an "Internal AI Transformation" team building agentic workflows with Claude, n8n and the company's own agents. This is substantive AI work, not marketing.

## Section 9: Regulated Industry

Not a classically regulated sector, but there is real compliance surface: **EU AI Act** (Art. 50 transparency and labelling for synthetic audio; GPAI obligations as a foundation-model provider), **GDPR** (voice data can be biometric data under Art. 9), deepfake and voice-cloning misuse (a dedicated Safety team, "Free Tier Abuse" engineering, AI-speech classifier), and US public-sector certifications (FedRAMP, GovRAMP, CJIS, CMMC under way per the Compliance Engineer posting). The engineering impact is concentrated in Safety, Security and Compliance teams. Product engineers ship fast ("rapid experimentation, minimal bureaucracy") with no formal safety cases.

**Verdict: PASS** (low burden for product engineering)

No posting among the 167 mentions on-call, pager duty or 24/7. The product, however, is real-time voice infrastructure and enterprise voice agents that customers run around the clock. **Verdict: CAUTION (team-dependent; UNVERIFIED).** Ask explicitly about incident response and rotations, especially for Agents - Core and Back-End roles.

**Typical engineering process (from 2025-2026 SWE, Full-Stack and Senior SWE reports):**
1. Online assessment (CoderPad or take-home): 2 medium and 1 medium-hard **LeetCode-style** problems in ~90 minutes, sometimes frontend questions as well.
2. Recruiter screen focused on motivation ("What excites you about ElevenLabs?"). Candidates are expected to know the product deeply.
3. Live coding (algorithmic with a practical twist, e.g. "merging sorted lists with dropping duplicates", async DB processing; "you need both high algorithmic skills and python sophisticated coding").
4. System design (e.g. "design voice chat for retail product search", "e-commerce shop with AI agent integration", permission tree). Reviewers say they "want something simple".
5. Manager or team calibration, then a final call with the CTO or a co-founder.

The process is **LeetCode-heavy** at the front, then design and fit. Recurring complaints: rejection without feedback even after positive technical rounds, "no headcount" after completing the full loop, interviewer mismatch (frontend engineer interviewing for senior backend), ghosting after "headcount complete", and a rushed founder round. Hires who accepted describe the process as challenging but well organised.

## Section 13: Verdict

**Recommendation: Exclude (do not actively pursue); monitor only for a German entity being founded.**

**Strengths:** ElevenLabs is the category leader in voice AI, with real in-house research and some of the fastest revenue growth in European tech ($600M ARR, 175% YoY). Its investor base makes funding risk negligible, and three tender offers in a year make its options unusually liquid. The engineering stack - Python backend, GCP/GKE, PyTorch, agent platform. Engineers rate the talent density, autonomy and pay highly, and remote work from Germany is explicitly permitted.

**Hard blockers:**
1. **No direct German employment contract can be confirmed.** There is no German GmbH (NorthData, privacy policy), so a Germany-based engineer would be employed by a foreign group company (possibly the Irish "Global Employment" entity) or by an EOR.
2. **Work-life balance fails the criteria.** Evening and weekend work is described as the norm by engineers in every year from 2024 to 2026 ("60+ hours is the norm", "Expect evening/weekend work", "Are you ready to work up to 10 hrs per day and during weekends?"). The company's own replies acknowledge the pace. The SWE-filtered WLB is 3.0.
3. **Not a mature, stable business for a long-term commitment.** The company is 4 years old, VC-backed and pre-IPO, valued at ~37× ARR, with no disclosed profitability. Reviewers flag commoditising models and a lagging Agents product. This is the opposite of the "mature, profitable" requirement.

**Secondary concerns:**
- A multi-year pattern of dismissals at or before the end of probation ("when in doubt, fire the person"), with no works council or local entity to counterbalance it.
- Leadership immaturity and favouritism reports, concentrated in GTM and FDE but also from a data engineer.
- German pay is unknown and location-adjusted. Option taxation without a German employer is complicated.
- De facto on-call for real-time agent infrastructure.
- A LeetCode-heavy interview with frequent "no feedback" rejections and headcount reversals.
- Many engineering openings are forward-deployed or deployment-strategist roles.

**What would need to change:** a German GmbH with direct contracts, and evidence (from the hiring manager or current German engineers) that a specific team works sustainable hours without on-call. Even then, maturity and stability would stay borderline until IPO. If an application is made anyway, for example to the Back-End Leaning or Agents - Core roles, verify before investing in the interview loop: (a) the legal employer and contract law, (b) the German salary band and option grant, (c) expected weekly hours and weekend or incident duties, (d) probation practice.
