# Snke OS GmbH - Employer Research Report

_Researched: 2026-05-28_

---

## Company Overview

Snke OS GmbH is a Munich-based B2B health tech platform company, commercially registered as HRB 258098 with the Munich commercial register. Office address: Weihenstephaner Straße 12, 81673 Munich (Berg am Laim district). The company was founded in 2020 as a subsidiary of Brainlab (the Munich-based medical device / surgical navigation company). In June 2025, Snke completed a formal spin-off from the Brainlab Group and began operating as an independent entity. Brainlab retains a 6.84% residual stake and acts as a customer and strategic distributor for selected Snke technologies. Current headcount: approximately 350, with teams in Munich, Chicago, Heidelberg, San Diego, and Tel Aviv.

**Products:** Snke Data (healthcare data management), Snke OS (enterprise health platform), Snke Xplore (data exploration), SnkeXR (medical-grade augmented reality glasses for surgical guidance).

**Leadership:** Stefan Vilsmeier (Chairman - also CEO of Brainlab), Andreas Giese (Managing Director/CEO).

## Criteria Assessment

### German GmbH - PASS

Snke OS GmbH, HRB 258098, Amtsgericht München. Full German legal entity. Criterion met without reservation.

### Product Company, Not Consulting - PASS

Snke builds and sells its own B2B software platform and products. No consulting or pre-sales role element in engineering positions. The business model is product-led SaaS/platform.

### English-Speaking Environment - PASS (likely)

The company website, careers page, and all press releases are in English. Offices across the US and Israel reinforce an English-first working culture. No German-language requirement detected in past job postings.

### Glassdoor Score - FAIL

**Rating: 3.3 / 5.0** (10 reviews, Glassdoor company ID E4910915). This falls below the required 4.0 threshold.

- 71% would recommend to a friend
- Recurring review themes: below-market salary for Munich, management with poor communication skills, task distribution based on favoritism (including gender-based disparities noted explicitly), "antiquated management style," and extremely limited remote work options
- One review describes: "antiquated management style with extremely limited remote work, lack of transparency on promotion and salary increase criteria"
- Another flags: "tasks being distributed based on favoritism, with lower-level tasks often provided to women while men receive more interesting assignments"

### Kununu Score - NOT MEANINGFUL

**Rating: 4.2 / 5.0**, 100% Weiterempfehlung. However, this is based on only 2 reviews. Statistically meaningless; cannot be used to override the Glassdoor signal.

### Profitability / Not VC-Subsidized - FAIL (HIGH RISK)

Snke operated as a Brainlab subsidiary from 2020 to mid-2025, meaning its costs were effectively backstopped by Brainlab's balance sheet. Since the June 2025 spinoff, Snke is an independent entity with no publicly disclosed funding round, no public revenue figures, and an explicit statement in the spinoff announcement that independence "allows Snke to attract new collaborators" - language that signals active fundraising intent rather than established profitability.

In short: Snke has just transitioned from a corporate-backed startup to what may become a VC-funded growth company. This is the opposite of the "profitable, steady-strategy, no longer VC-subsidized" profile preferred. The financial outlook is opaque and the stability risk is real.

### Salary Level - FAIL

No Snke-specific data on levels.fyi. Glassdoor reviewers explicitly call out **below-average compensation for Munich**. Given the  consistent negative salary signal in reviews, there is a meaningful risk that Snke does not meet market salary median - particularly given that they are a newly independent, pre-funding-round company under financial pressure.

### Commute - BORDERLINE / MARGINAL FAIL

Snke's office is at Weihenstephaner Straße 12, 81673 Munich, in the Berg am Laim district. The nearest public transport is U2 Josephsburg (~9 min walk) and S-Bahn Berg am Laim (~9 min walk).

### Remote Work - FAIL

Multiple Glassdoor reviews explicitly describe remote work as "extremely limited." This is a recurring, not isolated, complaint and applies to the Munich office specifically. Not compatible with the work-life balance preference.

### Role Type and Tech Stack - PARTIAL MATCH (with concerns)

Snke operates in health tech AI and data analytics, which maps to the preferred domain (AI/ML engineering, platform engineering). However, the tech stack includes a significant C++ component - job postings have listed C++17, C#, Go, Python, and TypeScript. Several engineering roles at Snke historically had C++ as a primary requirement. Python does appear, particularly on the data and platform side, but it is not the dominant language across all engineering roles. Any role here would need to be screened carefully for language split.

Additional concern: SnkeXR (augmented reality surgical glasses) is hardware-adjacent. Engineering roles supporting the XR platform may involve hardware integration work to be avoided.

### Stability - CONCERN

Snke is a 5-year-old company that was internally funded until mid-2025. As of the research date it is less than 1 year old as an independent entity, with unknown revenue, unknown profitability, and a management team under a chairman (Vilsmeier) who also runs Brainlab. The spinoff announcement frames the independence as enabling new investment - which is a stability risk flag, not a reassurance. Repeating a situation where a company hits a funding wall is exactly the scenario to avoid.

## Conclusion

Snke fails on four criteria simultaneously: Glassdoor score, profitability/stability, salary, and remote work policy. The commute is borderline. Beyond the hard fails, the management culture signals in the reviews - favoritism, poor communication, lack of transparency on promotions - are the exact pattern flagged as unacceptable in the employer criteria. The company is also at a precarious inflection point: freshly independent, seeking outside investment, with no confirmed profitable revenue base.

There is nothing in the research that warrants any exception to the criteria. **Do not pursue.**
