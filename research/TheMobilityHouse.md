# The Mobility House - Full Research Report

_Researched: 2026-06-02_


> **Critical Disqualifiers**
> 1. **Not profitable / still VC-backed**: The company raised a €50M Series C in November 2022 and an additional undisclosed round from RheinEnergie in April 2024. Balance sheet total has been shrinking (€48.8M in 2021 → €24.1M in 2022 → €20M in 2023), consistent with ongoing cash burn. No evidence of profitability anywhere.
> 2. **Below Glassdoor threshold**: 3.9 overall (threshold is 4.0), though Kununu is healthy.
> 3. **Salary below Munich staff SWE market**: Posted range €80K-€120K; Glassdoor comp & benefits rated 3.0/5 with reviews explicitly calling out "salary below Munich standards."

The full report follows for completeness.

### Business Model

The Mobility House develops and sells charging and energy management software (ChargePilot) deployed at fleet and commercial EV charging sites (25,000+ charge points, 2,700+ sites, 20 countries). It also operates in Vehicle-to-Grid (V2G) / bidirectional charging and energy trading / battery energy storage systems (BESS). Revenue model is software + hardware integration + services. The company bridges automotive and energy sectors.

The Mobility House's core products are ChargePilot (charging and energy management software) and energy trading platforms for BESS (battery energy storage systems) and V2G (vehicle-to-grid). "AI" appears in one job description as a nice-to-have skill, not a primary technology. The company uses optimization algorithms for load management and energy dispatch (BESS trading) but these are operations research / quant methods rather than deep learning or ML platform engineering. - Dedicated ML/AI teams

- AI model productization work (training pipelines, model deployment, inference optimization)

The "AI" framing is marketing-adjacent rather than engineering-substantive at this company.

## Section 10: On-Call

Job descriptions do not explicitly mention on-call. However, the BESS Tech Team role includes: "Monitor systems proactively" and the company espouses a DevOps "build it, ship it, run it" philosophy. Infrastructure supporting 25,000+ live charge points and active energy trading creates operational continuity requirements. On-call cannot be ruled out - the nature of the product (always-on charging infrastructure, real-time energy trading) makes this a realistic concern for at least some engineering roles, particularly SRE/Cloud Platform and potentially the BESS/energy trading teams.

**Verdict: UNVERIFIED / CAUTION (team-dependent)** - Recommend asking about on-call expectations for each role explicitly.

## Section 13: Verdict

**Recommendation: Exclude.**

The Mobility House is an interesting company in a genuinely compelling domain (energy transition, EV/V2G), with an interesting tech stack (Python primary, Rust nice-to-have, AWS cloud backend). WLB is exceptional (4.8) and the culture signals are clean - no toxic leadership, no hierarchy complaints, no overtime culture.

**Hard blockers:**

1. **Not profitable / still VC-backed.** The Mobility House raised Series C in 2022, took an additional undisclosed round from RheinEnergie in April 2024, and its balance sheet shrank from €48.8M to €20M in two years. This is a company still burning through investor cash, not a stable profitable employer.

2. **Salary below expectations.** The advertised €80K-€120K range is the Munich market median-to-75th-percentile for senior SWEs generally. Glassdoor comp rated 3.0/5 and declining; reviewers explicitly flag it as below Munich standards. Without equity that is realistically liquid, there is no total-comp mechanism to close this gap.

3. **No genuine AI/ML engineering content.** The Mobility House's roles are operational backend engineering for charging infrastructure and energy trading. AI appears as a nice-to-have keyword, not a core engineering domain.

**Secondary concerns:**

- Glassdoor 3.9 just misses the 4.0 threshold; the review volume is too low for the score to be statistically reliable in either direction.
- The German B2 requirement on at least one role posting creates uncertainty.
- On-call risk is unverified for infrastructure/BESS roles given the "build it, run it" DevOps philosophy.
- Company size (~300 people) limits career progression for a staff engineer; the title structure doesn't appear to go above Senior.

No further research is warranted unless The Mobility House reaches profitability, significantly raises its compensation bands, and opens a dedicated AI/ML engineering function.
