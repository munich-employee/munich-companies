# OMMAX - Full Research Report

_Researched: 2026-11-26_

> **Disqualifiers**
> 1. **Consulting firm.** OMMAX calls itself "a leading European AI-first consultancy", its registered purpose is "management consulting, transaction consulting as well as development and implementation of strategies", it was ranked among "Germany's Best Management Consulting Firms 2026" (Handelsblatt Research Institute), and it uses consultant career titles (Consultant, Senior Consultant, Manager, Director, VP, Partner). Engineers build client deliverables on client engagements (Forward Deployed AI Engineers plus a backend/platform layer that supports them), not an owned product.
> 2. **Work-life balance is the weakest score everywhere.** Glassdoor WLB is 2.7 for Germany/Munich and 3.0 globally. Reviews from 2024-2026 repeatedly describe long hours, late evenings and working during off-sites.
>

### Software-Centric Assessment

**FAIL.** There is no software product. The "Tech, Data & AI" unit builds client solutions ("enterprise AI agent ecosystems", "executive AI pilots", "AI-powered due diligence", "knowledge platforms") on engagements. Even the most product-like posting, Senior Backend Engineer - Agentic Systems, exists to "enable our Forward Deployed AI Engineers (FDEs) to turn agentic workflows into secure, reliable, measurable, and reusable customer solutions" and to "accelerate each new FDE deployment". Reusable "OMMAX assets" are delivery accelerators, not a sold product. About 120 of ~400 staff are AI/data specialists (Maddyness); the majority are strategy and transaction consultants.

## Section 13: Verdict

**Exclude.** OMMAX is a profitable, growing Munich consultancy with real applied-AI engineering work, but it fails the core criteria of being a software company and not a consultancy.

**Strengths:** Consistently profitable (€5.68M net income 2024) with a strong growth story; Munich headquarters in Maxvorstadt within walking distance of four U-Bahn lines; English-language engineering roles; genuinely current AI work (LLM agents, RAG, evaluation, MLOps) backed by OpenAI Select and Anthropic Select partner status and a new Chief AI Officer; Python-centric stack; Kununu Top Company six years running; well-regarded CEO (98-100% approval).

**Hard blockers:**

1. **Consulting, not software.** The company defines itself as "a leading European AI-first consultancy"; its registered purpose is management and transaction consulting; engineers deploy solutions into "real client landscapes" on engagements, and the most senior engineering role (Director Data & AI Engineering) owns "staffing, profitability" and "business development". There is no product to own and the career track is the consultant grid.
2. **Work-life balance.** Glassdoor WLB is 2.7 in Germany/Munich (3.0 globally), the lowest category on every filter, and "long hours", "long nights" and "regular overtime are expected" recur across Glassdoor and Kununu from 2024 to 2026.
3. **Compensation below the senior market.** Engineering data points (€57-73K for data/full-stack roles) and the Senior Consultant band (€64-75K) sit below the Munich SWE median of €86.8K and far below the 90th percentile (€134K). Only Manager/Director/VP (people and sales roles) reach €100K+. No IC track exists above Senior.

**Secondary concerns:**

- PE LBO (Eurazeo, July 2025) with profit transfer to the acquisition vehicle, a merger in integration (Singulier, May 2026) and managing-director churn in 2026.
- No works council or collective agreement found, alongside reports of bonus disputes on exit and opaque bonus calculation.
- The only engineering-specific review describes non-technical leaders deciding complex Data & AI projects and experienced senior data consultants leaving (one intern's view, Nov 2026, unverified).
- Hybrid model with desk sharing and office-presence pressure; production-support duties for client systems without a defined on-call policy.

**Recommendation:** Do not apply. No further research is warranted, since the consulting business model is structural and will not change under PE ownership aimed at scaling a consulting platform. The only thing that would change this assessment is OMMAX spinning out a standalone software product company (the careers page mentions "innovative spin-offs") with its own engineering organisation, product ownership and market-rate pay.
