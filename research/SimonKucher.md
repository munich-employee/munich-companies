# Simon-Kucher - Full Research Report

_Researched: 2026-10-15_

> **Critical Disqualifiers**
> 1. **Management consulting firm.** Simon-Kucher describes itself as "an international strategy and management consulting firm" (legal notice). The main German GmbH's registered purpose is management and marketing consulting, the career ladder is Consultant / Senior Consultant / Manager / Partner, and revenue comes from client engagements. This is a hard exclusion criterion. **FAIL.**
> 2. **The software arm is too small to offset this.** Software is built by Simon-Kucher Engine GmbH, a separately registered Bonn subsidiary with **13 employees (FY2024)**. Its product engineering roles are advertised as hybrid out of the **Berlin** office. The other German engineering role (Senior DevOps, Azure) is internal IT support for the consulting business.
>
> The full report is completed below for completeness.

### Business Model

Simon-Kucher sells strategy consulting on growth, pricing, monetisation, marketing and sales, plus private-equity work (due diligence, value creation). Its brand is world-leading pricing expertise. The 2025 growth drivers it named were pricing/monetisation, investor work, data & AI, and "digital and software-enabled commercial solutions, sold under the Engine brand". Technology work sits in four units:

- **Simon-Kucher Engine GmbH** (launched October 2021, "Simon-Kucher-as-a-Service"): pricing software suites, namely SK Pricer (Products, Deals, Promotions, TradeStrat, RAISE, PricePro), Dynamica (Retail, SmartRates, Deposits), Loyalty and LS Genius (life-sciences reference pricing). It also offers "Tailored Pricing, Marketing & Sales Apps (solution design and rapid software development)", which are custom client builds. Clients include Metro, makro, Stora Enso, Center Parcs, and several Volksbanks and Sparkassen. Mitschke: Engine "is the vehicle for transforming Simon-Kucher recommendations, know-how, and best practices". A €500M ambition was stated at launch. In FY2024 Engine GmbH had 13 employees, €2.98M total assets and €0.61M net income.
- **SKAI Lab**: founded in 2025, builds AI/GenAI products "offered as Software-as-a-Service".
- **Central Data & AI function**: builds internal GenAI productivity tools for the ~2,000+ staff.
- **Elevate** (data analytics consulting) and the **AI Engineering practice** in Warsaw, which delivers "enterprise AI, Agentic AI" to clients.

### Software-Centric Assessment

Revenue is overwhelmingly consulting fees. Engine is the only legal entity whose purpose is software: "Entwicklung und Vertrieb von cloudbasierten, KI-gestützten Preisoptimierungs- und kommerziellen Softwarelösungen sowie Bereitstellung dazugehöriger Implementierungs-, Integrations- und Beratungsleistungen". Even here, implementation and consulting services are bundled in. With 13 staff and €3M total assets against a €606M group, software is a minor add-on to the consulting business. A 2021 Glassdoor review from a software engineer captures the culture: "Software development is only a department, hence the overall work environment is not developer-first". **FAIL.**

### Consulting-Adjacency Assessment

- **Self-identification:** Yes. Every job posting, including the engineering ones, opens with "Simon-Kucher is a global consultancy with more than 2,200 employees in 30+ countries". The legal notice reads "Simon-Kucher is an international strategy and management consulting firm". It is ranked in brand eins/Statista "Die besten Unternehmensberater 2026". A Kununu reviewer: "Es ist eine Unternehmensberatung."
- **Billing to clients:** Yes for the core business (project work, flying between offices). The Warsaw AI Engineering practice requires "willingness to travel to client sites as needed, depending on project requirements". The German Data Science & GenAI manager role requires presenting "to client teams and senior executives".
- **Consultant career track:** Yes. The ladder is Associate Consultant, Consultant, Senior Consultant, Manager, Senior Manager, Director, Partner. Kununu reviewers say careers for non-consultants are "kaum vorhanden" (almost nonexistent).
- **External client deliverable vs internal product:** Mixed. Engine builds a sellable SaaS pricing platform, which is product work, but it also does custom app builds for clients. The DevOps role supports internal IT and the Data & AI function.
- **Registered purpose** of the main German GmbH: "Management- und Marketingberatung im In- und Ausland sowie Transfer von Management-Know-how nach wissenschaftlichen Standards in Zusammenarbeit mit Hochschulinstituten und Hochschullehrern".

Consulting is the core business. The only product-engineering unit is a 13-person subsidiary. **Hard FAIL**, slightly softer than a pure MBB firm only because Engine is a genuine product unit.

## Section 13: Verdict

**Exclude.** Simon-Kucher is a profitable, steadily growing, partner-owned pricing consultancy with decent ratings: Kununu 4.0 with Top Company 2026, Glassdoor 4.0 in Munich. Its Munich office is a reasonable 36-38 minutes away. Its core business, however, is management consulting, and software is a small side unit.

**Strengths:** financial stability (€606M revenue, sustained growth, no layoffs), a world-leading pricing brand, a collegial international culture, Kununu Top Company 2026, and a real if small product unit (Engine) building pricing SaaS.

**Hard blockers:**
1. **Consulting firm.** The company self-identifies as "an international strategy and management consulting firm". The main German GmbH's registered purpose is "Management- und Marketingberatung", the career ladder runs from Consultant to Partner, and revenue is consulting fees. This fails the no-consulting criterion outright.
2. **Not software-centric.** Engine GmbH, the only software entity, had 13 employees and €3M total assets in FY2024, against a €606M group. A software engineer reviewer described the environment as "not developer-first", and Kununu reviewers say careers for non-consultants are "kaum vorhanden".
3. **No suitable Munich engineering role.**
   - The Engine Lead Software Engineer posting lists Munich, but it is "hybrid ... between working in our Berlin office and remote". It is also a Java-first lead role on AWS.
   - The Senior DevOps (Azure) role is internal IT with production incident management.
   - The Munich AI role (Manager Data Science & GenAI) is client-facing consulting and requires German.

**Secondary concerns:**
- Salary bands frozen for four years, with no equity, and no software engineer salary data anywhere.
- Partner politics and vague top-management communication.
- No overtime pay.
- Works council and collective agreement status unknown.
- Likely operational duty in the DevOps role.

**Recommendation:** Exclude, and keep tier C. Reconsider only if Engine grows into a substantial product organisation with a Munich engineering team and Python-centred individual-contributor roles. Further research is not warranted, because the consulting classification is structural and cannot be resolved in an interview.
