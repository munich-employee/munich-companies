# Cognizant - Full Research Report

_Researched: 2026-11-05_

> **Critical Disqualifiers**
>
> 1. **Consulting / IT services.** Cognizant is an IT services and outsourcing firm. Engineers are billed to client engagements, work models are defined as "3 days a week in a client or Cognizant office", and the German entity's registered purpose opens with "technische und Software-Beratung". This is the business model, not an adjacent activity.
> 2. **Compensation far below the Munich senior bracket.** Glassdoor Germany: Senior Software Engineer €58K-€74K (13 submissions); levels.fyi Munich data points €68K-€75K. The Munich senior median on levels.fyi is ~€95K-€100K.
> 3. **German required on most Munich-area engineering roles.** Cognizant Mobility (the Munich engineering subsidiary) asks for "fließende Deutschkenntnisse"; several Cognizant GmbH postings require B1 to C1 German.
>
> The full report follows for completeness.

### Business Model

Cognizant sells IT services on contracts: application development and maintenance, cloud and infrastructure management, business process outsourcing, SAP / Salesforce / Workday implementation, and (since the 2021 ESG Mobility and 2024 Belcan acquisitions) engineering R&D services. Revenue is reported by client industry (Financial Services, Health Sciences, Products & Resources, Communications/Media/Technology). It is the archetypal offshore-leveraged IT services firm: ~71% of staff sit in India.

### Software-Centric Assessment

Cognizant builds software, but for clients, under statements of work. Engineers do not own a product codebase over time; they deliver project scope, then move to the next engagement ("This may change based on the project you're engaged in, as well as business and client requirements", German Frontier Engineer posting). Internal platforms (Neuro AI, Agent Foundry, Flowsource) exist, but they are delivery accelerators sold with services, not a product line that the German teams build. **FAIL.**

Assessment: genuine applied AI work exists, but it is client delivery and pre-sales (AI Chief Architect owns "pitch decks, one-pagers, demo scripts"). Much of the German AI headcount sits in sales/architecture rather than hands-on engineering.

## 13. Verdict

**Exclude.**

Cognizant is a large, profitable, GPTW-certified company with reasonable work-life balance in Germany, remote-friendly policies (especially at Cognizant Mobility), and real applied agentic-AI work through its Frontier pods and the Anthropic and Google Cloud partnerships. It has two registered German employers, one of them headquartered in Munich.

**Hard blockers:**

1. **Consulting / IT services business model.** The German entity's registered purpose is "technische und Software-Beratung", engineers are staffed onto client engagements ("3 days a week in a client or Cognizant office", "may change based on the project you're engaged in"), and the career ladder uses consulting titles. Cognizant Mobility likewise delivers client projects for automotive OEMs.
2. **Not software-centric.** No internal product that German engineers build and own; internal platforms are delivery accelerators.
3. **Compensation well below the bracket.** Glassdoor Germany Senior Software Engineer €58K-€74K, levels.fyi Munich €68K-€75K, no equity. The levels.fyi Munich Senior SWE median is ~€95K-€100K and the 90th percentile ~€155K; Cognizant's senior band sits below even the Munich all-levels median (€82K).

**Secondary concerns:**

- German required for most Cognizant Mobility engineering roles and several client-facing GmbH roles.
- Project Leap restructuring (2026), offshoring of German roles, and a reported promotion freeze.
- No confirmed works council or collective agreement.
- On-call depends on the engagement; managed-services and 24/7 DevOps engagements exist.
- Kununu 3.3 is well below the consulting sector average (3.9).

**Recommendation:** Exclude. The consulting model and a senior pay band roughly €25K-€40K below the Munich median are structural, not team-specific; no role or team choice would change them, so further research is not warranted.
