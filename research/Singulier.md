# Singulier - Full Research Report

_Researched: 2026-11-26_

> **Disqualifiers**
> 1. **Consulting firm.** Singulier (trading name of Squadigital SAS, Paris) calls itself "an AI, technology and data consultancy firm supporting global corporate brands and private equity firms". Its French registered activity is NAF 70.22Z "Conseil pour les affaires et autres conseils de gestion". Its Munich GmbH is registered for "due diligence and strategy consulting services". The career ladder is Analyst / Consultant / Senior Consultant / Project Leader / Senior Manager / Associate Director / Director / Partner, and engineers ("Forward Deployed Engineers") build client deliverables on engagements.
> 2. **French is a primary requirement.** Every current engineering and Tech & AI posting (Senior Forward Deployed Engineer, Technology & AI Senior Consultant, Technology & AI Senior Manager) requires "Fluency in French and English".
> 3. **No engineering presence in Munich.** All 11 open roles are in Paris. The Munich team is a small partner-led due-diligence and strategy group, and since the May 2026 combination the website gives OMMAX's office (Kaulbachstraße 4) as the Munich address. LinkedIn lists no Germany cluster among its 103 associated members (France 71, UK 18).
>
> Since July 2026 Singulier has been owned by OMMAX's Eurazeo LBO vehicle. For a Munich-based engineering role in this group, see `research/OMMAX.md`.
>
> The full report follows for completeness.

## Section 13: Verdict

**Exclude.** Singulier is a Paris-headquartered strategy and transaction-advisory consultancy for private equity, now part of the OMMAX group, and it fails on several hard criteria for a Munich-based senior engineering role.

**Strengths:** A respected PE-focused boutique with a strong growth record (17 to ~100 people in seven years, "Technology Advisory Firm of the Year" 2026), high early-career learning value, and a modern, opinionated Python stack for its new forward-deployed team (FastAPI, Pydantic AI, Polars, DuckDB, LanceDB, evaluations and tracing on every AI app). The new CTO has a product and data-platform background, and the delivery team is small enough for a senior engineer to shape standards. There is no on-call, and regulatory overhead is low.

**Hard blockers:**

1. **Consulting, not software.** The business sells due diligence, strategy and transformation projects. Engineers deploy solutions at clients and hand them over; Holis and Graphite are delivery assets, not products. The registered purposes in France and Munich are consulting.
2. **French is required.** "Fluency in French and English" appears on the Senior Forward Deployed Engineer, Tech & AI Senior Consultant and Senior Manager postings; clients, reviews and interviews are predominantly French.
3. **No engineering role in Munich.** All 11 open roles are in Paris. The Munich GmbH exists for partner-led deal work, and the Munich office is now OMMAX's.

**Secondary concerns:**

- Work-life balance is the recurring complaint across years and roles, including from a Data engineer (2023), and the only post-acquisition reviews (Sep 2026) are the weakest so far.
- Fresh LBO ownership (Eurazeo via Gaïa (Germany) Newco GmbH since July 2026), integration into OMMAX with the brand's future undefined, and Munich hires from 2025 no longer listed.
- No works council or collective agreement on the German side; reports of bonuses "not in line with contracts".
- Salary data for engineers is absent, and the group's Munich grid sits below the Munich market median.
- Thin review base (21 Glassdoor reviews, none from Germany; no Kununu or Blind).

**Recommendation:** Do not pursue Singulier. No further research is warranted, since the consulting model and the French-language requirement would not change even if a Munich role appeared. For Munich roles in this group, OMMAX GmbH is the relevant employer, and `research/OMMAX.md` already covers it (also excluded as a consultancy). Singulier would only be worth revisiting if the group opened an English-only, Munich-based engineering role on an internal product platform with a defined IC ladder, which no current signal suggests.
