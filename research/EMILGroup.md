## Section 9: Regulated Industry

EMIL sells core systems to insurers regulated by **BaFin** (Germany) and **FINMA** (Switzerland). As a critical ICT third-party provider to insurers, EMIL falls under **DORA** (EU Digital Operational Resilience Act, applicable since Jan 2025) contractual requirements: incident reporting support, exit plans, audit rights, resilience testing, and outsourcing controls under VAG/BaFin VAIT. ## Section 13: Verdict

**Exclude (at most Monitor).** EMIL Group is a real, credible Berlin insurtech product company with marquee references (Swisscom, Wertgarantie), a modern Go/Python/TypeScript stack on AWS, English as the engineering language, and an applied LLM agent (EMILY) that gives the platform a genuine AI angle. Recent engineering reviews describe strong team spirit and transparent leads, and remote work within Germany is offered for senior engineering roles.

**Hard blockers:**
1. **No Munich office.** Berlin and Zurich only. The role is "remote-friendly with occasional trips to our Berlin office", so Munich would be a permanently remote position in an office-centred culture (weekly lunches, all-hands), with recurring ~4h-each-way travel.
2. **Financial fragility.** At the last filed balance sheet (31.12.2024) the company held about **€1K in cash** against **€5.33M liabilities**, with €4.84M accumulated losses. The €355K FY2024 profit is accompanied by €5.0M of capitalised development, so it is not cash profit. Total equity raised is only ~€5.2M, with no disclosed institutional round since 2020. The base-case runway model gives 1-2 years and depends on an undisclosed 2026 capital injection.
3. **Review floors cannot be verified.** Glassdoor has a single review. Kununu is **3.9** (below the 4.0 insurance-industry average) with **71%** recommendation on 24 reviews, and includes a reported unpaid-overtime pattern ("9-11 Stunden Tage ... ohne Vergütung oder zeitausgleich").

**Secondary concerns:**
- Salary is unpublished and the proxies (PM ~€69K, startup "normal" pay, VSOP-only equity) point below the large-corporate Munich bracket.
- Likely on-call for senior platform engineers on a multi-tenant SaaS system under DORA obligations, unconfirmed.
- No works council, plus a first-hand account (sales, May 2026) of a dismissal one month after raising a leadership conflict.
- Customer-implementation pressure: reviewers mention overselling the product and absorbing customer frustration.
- Small, very young team; all-male leadership; 2024 "intransparent management" reviews; the single interview review describes an unstructured three-hour session.

**Recommendation:** Do not prioritise. What would change this: (a) a disclosed 2026 funding round or evidence of cash-flow breakeven, (b) a published salary band for Senior/Leading Software Engineer at or above ~€95-100K base for a Germany-remote hire, and (c) written confirmation of remote-from-Munich with limited travel and no on-call (or compensated on-call). If those three were confirmed, it would move to Monitor; in an interview, ask specifically about funding status and runway, the 2026 capital increase, the engineering salary band, on-call rotation, and how often remote engineers are expected in Berlin.
