### Business Model

SIMCON sells Cadmould, a simulation suite for plastic injection moulding, to manufacturers in automotive, aerospace, medical technology, electronics and packaging. Recent moves: Cadmould Flex (monthly subscription instead of perpetual licences), the Cadmould AI Solver (March 2026, a transformer-based "Large Engineering Model" co-developed with Emmi AI and trained on decades of its own solver output), and the "Agentic Molding" trademarks filed in October 2026. A French subsidiary (SIMCON SARL, Villeurbanne) handles that market. The registered purpose also mentions consulting, but the company sells its own product; it is not a consultancy.

## Section 6: AI/ML & Tech Stack

#### AI Strategy

Genuine applied AI rather than marketing:
- **Cadmould AI Solver** (launched 18 March 2026): a transformer-based neural physics model co-developed with Emmi AI, trained on decades of output from SIMCON's own numerical solver. The company claims results up to 1,000x faster than classical solvers for early design exploration, with Cadmould Flex kept for final validation. It is offered as a free browser research preview and has "already shipped to our first customers". These claims come from the company's own releases and have not been independently benchmarked.
- **Agentic Molding Toolkit** and the trademarks "Agentic Molding" / "CADMOULD Agentic Molding" (filed 9 October 2026).
- **Patents:** two computer-implemented optimisation methods (October 2024, April 2026).
- **Partner Program** with early access to shrinkage and warpage prediction.

The open ML Engineer role builds the platform around these models: training-data and model versioning with lineage, model registry and promotion, drift and degradation monitoring, and the AWS service that serves the models to customers.

#### Tech Stack

Derived from the current ML Engineer posting (LinkedIn 4482823978, Personio) and the careers page; no public engineering blog or GitHub organisation was found.

| Category | Technologies |
|---|---|
| **Primary language(s)** | Python (for the ML platform role); the language of the core Cadmould solver is not published |

**Summary:** Python-centric ML platform engineering (model lifecycle, data versioning, serving) on AWS, built around an in-house transformer physics model, inside a long-established simulation-software company. Applied production engineering, not research.

## Section 13: Verdict

**Recommendation: Actively pursue the ML Engineer, MLOps & Platform role, with two points to settle in the first conversation.**

**Strengths.** SIMCON is a 38-year-old product company, not a startup, with friendly and flexible reviews and no toxicity signal. The role is concrete and production-oriented: own the training-data and model lifecycle, the registry, drift monitoring and the AWS service for a model that has already shipped to customers. It can be done fully remote from Munich. The interview process is three conversations, including a technical one that digs into real work, with no algorithm or system-design gauntlet described.

**Hard blockers:** none found.

**Secondary concerns:**
1. **Financial stability:** about −€0.8M in 2024 after Fortino took 76.5% in June 2024. With ~40 staff, a repeat of that loss would be material, so continuity depends on the AI bet working and on Fortino's continued support.
2. **On-call:** "builds and runs the platform" in a small team with no SRE function; no rota is stated.
3. **Pay:** no data, the posting asks for 3+ years, and the one critical review is about pay lagging inflation. Expect a band below large Munich employers.
4. **Culture language:** English is the working language, but the company calls itself "mostly German-speaking", so some social and company-wide communication will be in German.
5. **Level:** pitched at 3+ years; confirm the level and the growth path ("take on more ownership as the platform and the team grow").

**What to verify in the process:** the salary band; whether running the AWS service includes any out-of-hours duty; 2025 results and the runway Fortino has committed; how often on-site visits to Würselen are expected; and team size and structure (AI engineers, data scientists, the cloud team).
