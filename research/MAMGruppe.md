# MAM Gruppe - Full Research Report

_Researched: 2026-11-17_

> **Critical Disqualifiers**
> 1. **Not an employer of software engineers: MAM Gruppe is a recruitment agency.** LinkedIn classifies it as "Staffing and Recruiting"; the German GmbH's registered purpose is "Vermittlung von Arbeitskräften" (placement of workers); the UK parent contracts with clients on "Contingent Search" terms with a fee of 30% of the placed candidate's first-year total remuneration. All seven software/data engineering ads currently under its LinkedIn name are for undisclosed clients ("Our client is seeking...", "Get in touch with the MAM Gruppe team for a confidential conversation about this role"). The only role MAM itself hires for is recruitment consultant, on-site in Twickenham, UK.
> 2. **No engineering presence in Munich.** The "Munich office" is a Regus serviced-office centre at the Munich Airport Center (Terminalstraße Mitte 18), held by MAM Gruppe Germany GmbH, a €25,000 GmbH entered in the Munich register on 3 July 2025. LinkedIn locates 51 of its 129 members in the UK and 10 in India; Germany is not among the top five locations.
> 3. **Systemic toxic-leadership pattern in its own reviews (2021-2026)**: owner-centred control, commission withheld or deferred, five days a week in the office, and allegations of sexism, racism and bullying. This concerns MAM's own staff, not an engineer placed with a client, but it is the agency that would represent a candidate.
>
> **Practical implication:** a MAM Gruppe posting points to a different company. The real employer must be identified (ask the recruiter for the client name before agreeing to any CV submission) and researched on its own. This report cannot assess that employer.
>
> The full report follows for completeness.

### Business Model

A London-run recruitment firm that places mid-to-senior professionals exclusively with employers in Germany, in three divisions: Technology (Data & AI, Cyber Security, Software Engineering, Cloud & Infrastructure, Product & Digital Transformation, SAP), Legal & Compliance, and Finance, plus "Corporate Functions" and a Contract & Freelance desk. Three delivery modes: contingent and executive search, "Strategic Hiring Projects" for volume hiring, and contract placements of Freiberufler or workers "placed under AÜG frameworks". It claims 350+ clients from scale-ups to DAX-40 corporations and a "97% candidate retention rate"; named case studies are CompuGroup Medical, Commerzbank, MISUMI, SSI SCHAEFER and ZEISS Digital Partners.

Commercial terms (mamgruppe.com Terms and Conditions of Business, contracting party MAM Gruppe Limited): "No fee is payable unless and until a Candidate Introduced by the Company is Engaged by the Client"; the "Introduction Fee will equate to 30% of the Candidate's commencing total taxable remuneration ... (includes fixed salary, variable/bonus, and company car)". An "Introduction" is "the provision to the Client of information by the Company which identifies a candidate", so a fee claim attaches as soon as a CV or name is passed to the client.

### Software-Centric Assessment

There is nothing to assess as an engineering employer. MAM employs recruiters ("Account Manager", "Recruitment Consultant", "Principal Consultant", "Sales Director" are the Glassdoor titles), not engineers. Its website is built by a third-party recruitment web agency (footer link to recruiterweb.co.uk; Next.js assets). The privacy policy says it uses "technology tools, including automated processing tools, for administrative tasks such as formatting candidate materials", i.e. CVs may be reformatted before submission.

### Consulting-Adjacency Assessment

Recruitment is a client-service business and MAM fits every consulting-adjacency marker:
- **Self-identification:** UK parent's registered SIC code is 70229 "Management consultancy activities other than financial management"; sibling companies are SIC 78109 "Other activities of employment placement agencies".
- **Billing:** revenue is per-placement fees (30% of first-year remuneration) and contractor margins, billed to external clients.
- **Titles:** the career track is Consultant → Senior Recruitment Consultant → Principal Consultant → Director.
- **Deliverable:** candidates for external clients; engineers are the product being placed, not staff. Contract placements (AÜG or freelance) would put an engineer on a client's project with MAM as intermediary.

## Section 13: Verdict

**Exclude MAM Gruppe as an employer.** It is a UK-run recruitment agency, and its Munich presence is a 2025 placement GmbH registered at a serviced-office centre at Munich Airport. It is not a place where software engineers work, so no further research on MAM as an employer is warranted.

**Strengths:** It has real reach into the German market: 60 live LinkedIn ads, named clients such as Commerzbank, CompuGroup Medical and ZEISS Digital Partners, and directors for Software, Data & AI and Cloud. Its contingent model costs the candidate nothing. Recruiters who like a high-commission sales environment rate the training and social life well.

**Hard blockers:**
1. **Not an engineering employer.** Every engineering role under its name belongs to an undisclosed client; MAM only hires recruiters, on-site in Twickenham.
2. **Not software-centric and consulting-adjacent.** Revenue is per-placement fees (30% of first-year remuneration) from external clients; registered as management consultancy (UK) and labour placement (Germany).
3. **No Munich engineering office.** The address is a Regus centre in Oberding, about 30 km north of Munich, with no identifiable MAM staff there.

**Secondary concerns (relevant if MAM is used as a channel to a client):**
- The agency's own culture is rated HIGH risk for toxicity (2021-2026), with commission and pay delays attributed to "cash flow"; combined UK cash was about £96K at end-2025, and the UK group is about £0.3m net negative once intercompany balances are netted.
- Most of its Germany-based ads, including all current Munich engineering ads, require fluent German.
- A 30% fee attaches to any introduction, and MAM's terms allow a fee claim as soon as identifying information is passed to a client.

**Recommendation:** Treat MAM Gruppe postings as leads, not employers. Before agreeing to any submission, get the client's name, check it against companies already applied to directly (to avoid a duplicate introduction and a fee conflict), and research that client under this template. Ask the recruiter to confirm in writing that no CV will be sent without explicit, per-role consent. The only current MAM engineering ads that do not require German are the remote Senior Backend Engineer (Node.js/PHP, small voice-AI team, likely on-call), the Berlin Julia Developer and a Berlin data-science contract; none is a Munich role.
