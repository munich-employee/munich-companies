# Nagarro - Full Research Report

_Researched: 2026-11-03_

> **Critical Disqualifiers** (report continued in full for completeness):
> 1. **Consulting / IT-services company.** Nagarro describes itself as "a global provider of digital engineering services ... to create digital solutions for its clients" (Joint Reasoned Statement, Aug 2026). Engineers are staffed on client projects and bill against them. The German careers page and German postings use consultant titles throughout ("Senior SAP CO Consultant", "Managing Consultant", "Principal Consultant").
> 2. **No German software engineering roles outside SAP, and German C1 is mandatory.** All 28 open German postings (SmartRecruiters, 3 Nov 2026) are SAP consulting/ABAP, product owner, sales or trainee roles. The engineering-flavoured ones require "High fluency in German (C1 or higher)". None names Munich as the work location.
> 3. **Change of control in progress.** Persistent Systems (Pune, India) holds ~94% of the shares after its EUR 81/share takeover offer closed on 6 Oct 2026; completion, delisting and a squeeze-out are expected by end of Q1 2027.
> 4. **Glassdoor 3.9 global / 3.9 Software Engineer / 3.8 Senior Software Engineer** (7,134 reviews), below a "strong Glassdoor score" bar.

### Business Model

Nagarro sells digital product engineering, ERP (SAP) consulting, managed services and "AI transformation" services to 1,000+ clients in 72 countries. Europe and North America are about three quarters of revenue, and Germany is the largest European market. Revenue is project-based or recurring service revenue (time-and-material and managed services); it scales with billable headcount and utilisation. Most delivery headcount sits in India.

In Germany the business has two parts:
- **Nagarro ES (Kronberg im Taunus, Freiburg, etc.)**: the former Allgeier Enterprise Services SAP house, ~1,200 people per its Kununu profile. This is where nearly all current German openings are.
- **Digital engineering**: the Munich-headquartered Nagarro Germany GmbH, which delivers custom software for German clients, largely through offshore/nearshore teams.

### Software-Centric Assessment

Nagarro writes software, but as a billable deliverable for whichever client buys it. There is no owned product whose quality is Nagarro's strategic moat; the "product" is engineering capacity. Reviewers describe the consequences directly: "Weiterbildung ist nicht im Fokus, die fakturierbaren Stunden sind immer höher priorisiert" (Kununu, Jun 2026), and "Man ist entweder nicht ausreichend ausgelastet und damit potenziell von Entlassung bedroht oder dauerhaft überlastet" (Glassdoor DE, Jan 2026). **FAIL.**

### Consulting-Adjacency Assessment

- **Self-identification:** Kununu industry category "Beratung/Consulting"; the website and service pages use "Digital Product Engineering and Technology Consulting"; Kununu employer text: "mit einem unbefristeten Arbeitsvertrag bei uns die Welt des Consultings kennenzulernen und schnell in Kundenprojekten Verantwortung zu übernehmen."
- **Billing hours to clients:** Yes. "fakturierbare Stunden" (billable hours) and utilisation appear in reviews; the earnings releases track utilisation and clients above EUR 1M.
- **Career track:** Consultant ladder in Germany (Associate Consultant, Consultant, Senior Consultant, Managing Consultant, Principal Consultant), plus the global engineer ladder (Associate Staff Engineer to Distinguished Engineer).
- **Deliverable:** External client deliverables. Postings say "customer-specific requirements are implemented within projects", "Engage with customers", "Flexibility and willingness to travel", "2nd and 3rd level support ... for existing customers."
- **Registered purpose:** Nagarro SE: "software and technology consulting, development, ... implementation, maintenance, operation". Nagarro Germany GmbH: "Implementation of software projects and software consulting".

**Verdict: consulting / IT-services company. Hard FAIL.**

## Section 6: AI/ML & Tech Stack

#### AI Strategy

Nagarro markets an "AI-native engineering" framework called **Fluidic Intelligence** (Fluidic Advisory, Fluidic Forge, Fluidic Teams), became an **OpenAI Services Partner** (Dec 2025, APAC focus: OpenAI API and ChatGPT Enterprise), and builds agentic systems on Databricks for clients. This is real applied-AI delivery work, but it is a **client service**, not an owned AI product. Postings confirm the pattern: "AI Agent Platform Engineer" (Romania/Poland, Oct 2026) is about building "agentic platforms, agent harnesses, and agent factories" for enterprise clients, with good-to-have experience in banking and mainframe integration. A global Glassdoor reviewer: "As per CEO, priority is AI and ML but 98% projects are without these opportunities." In Germany, AI/ML shows up only in two posting keyword hits, neither an engineering role.

## Section 13: Verdict

**Recommendation: Exclude.**

**Strengths.** Nagarro is profitable and cash-generative. Its Munich headquarters is about three minutes' walk from U3 Obersendling. It has genuine German employing entities with direct contracts, a generous work-from-anywhere policy, and a Kununu profile (4.3, 90% recommend, Top Company six years running) well above the consulting-industry average. German reviewers consistently like their colleagues and the flat hierarchy.

**Hard blockers:**
1. **Consulting company.** Nagarro sells engineering capacity to clients and staffs engineers on client projects. The career ladder uses consultant titles, postings require client travel, and reviews tie job security to billable utilisation. This fails the "no consultant/forward-deployed/solution-engineer type of arrangement" criterion outright.
2. **No matching role exists in Germany.** All 28 open German postings are SAP consulting/ABAP, product owner, sales or trainee roles. None is a Python-centred senior or staff software engineering role, and none is located in Munich.
3. **German C1 is a primary requirement** in every German engineering-type posting ("High fluency in German (C1 or higher)"; "Fluent German ... mandatory").
4. **Compensation is below market.** German software engineer data points (EUR 56-64K on Glassdoor and Kununu) sit below the Munich 25th percentile (EUR 68.1K), far from the Munich 75th percentile (EUR 104K). There is no meaningful equity, and the employee share programme will disappear with the delisting.

**Secondary concerns:**
- Change of control to Persistent Systems (~94%, closing ~Q1 2027). Its employee and location commitments are statements of intent only, and German reviewers already report growing India-led process and IT control.
- Glassdoor 3.9 global, 3.8 for Senior Software Engineers, with SWE recommendation down 6 points in 12 months.
- No works council and no collective agreement. Overtime "abgegolten" contracts and go-live weekend work in parts of the German business.
- Support duties (2nd/3rd level, hypercare) built into role descriptions.
- The 2025-26 accounting and short-seller episode was cleared of fraud but flagged governance weaknesses.

**Recommendation sentence.** Nagarro is a stable, well-liked German SAP and digital-engineering consultancy, but it is structurally the wrong kind of employer: consulting work, no Python-centred engineering roles in Germany, German C1 required, pay below market, and an ownership change in progress. No further research or application is warranted unless Persistent creates an owned-product engineering unit in Munich, which nothing currently suggests.
