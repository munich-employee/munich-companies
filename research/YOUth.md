## Section 2: Local Commercial Entity

| Entity | Registration | Address | Purpose |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Youth HealthTech Germany UG (haftungsbeschränkt)** | HRB 256372, Amtsgericht Berlin Charlottenburg | c/o Antler Innovation GmbH, Jägerstraße 32, 10117 Berlin | "Die Entwicklung, die Forschung und der Vertrieb von Produkten und Services im Healthcare-Bereich" |

The registered purpose explicitly covers development and research of healthcare products and services - this confirms engineering work (not just sales/distribution). The entity is registered c/o Antler's Berlin office, which is standard for Antler portfolio companies.

However, the legal form is a **UG (haftungsbeschränkt)** with only **€500 share capital**. A UG is the minimum viable German corporate form - it requires as little as €1 capital and is typically used as a placeholder by very early stage startups before converting to a GmbH (which requires €25K minimum capital). The company has not yet converted despite having raised $4.5M. This is not unusual for Antler portfolio companies early on, but it signals the company has not yet committed to the full corporate infrastructure of an established employer.

Job postings list Munich as the work location, but the only registered German entity is the Berlin UG. There is no separately registered Munich GmbH visible in company registries. The Munich office appears to be operational but not separately incorporated.

**Verdict:** PASS with significant CAUTION - German entity exists and purpose confirms engineering work, but UG form and €500 capital are unusually low for a company this far along, and the Munich operational presence is not matched by a separate Munich legal entity.

## Section 9: Regulated Industry

YOU(th) deliberately positions its app as a **wellness tool, not a diagnostic device**, specifically to avoid FDA/CE-IVD regulatory approval requirements. The company states this explicitly ("not a diagnostic device") to bypass the most burdensome regulatory pathways.

However, the domain (health biomarkers, claims about detecting cardiovascular/respiratory/metabolic risks) is adjacent to regulated medical devices and the regulatory boundary is fragile. Regulators (FDA, EU MDR) are actively scrutinizing wellness apps that make health claims. Future regulatory action could force the company to either pull claims or pursue full medical device approval - either outcome is materially disruptive.

**Verdict: CAUTION** - not currently regulated, but operating at the boundary of medical device regulation with fragile positioning.

## Section 13: Verdict

**Exclude.** YOU(th) is an interesting company from a technology perspective but fails multiple hard criteria simultaneously.

**Strengths:** The product is a genuinely ML-driven platform with real research backing. The AI/ML content of the work is authentic - this is applied ML (computer vision, voice biomarker analysis), not a company using "AI" as a marketing term. The mission (preventive health access) is compelling. Python is almost certainly the primary engineering language. The company does have a German legal entity.

**Hard blockers:**

1. **Not profitable - seed-stage VC-backed.** The candidate is explicitly looking for a long-term, stable employer with a steady strategy. YOU(th) is a 15-person seed-stage startup with ~$4.47M raised, no disclosed revenue, and an estimated 2-2.5 year runway before needing a Series A. This is the antithesis of stability.

2. **Compensation mismatch.** The only disclosed salary range (€70-100K for a comparable founding-level role) is substantially below what a Staff Software Engineer with 20 years of experience should expect in Munich (€130-180K+ total compensation). Even if engineering roles were priced modestly higher, the startup equity offered carries very high illiquidity risk.

3. **No matching roles currently open.** There is no Senior/Staff backend engineering, ML engineering, or AI/ML platform role open. The Full Stack Engineer role is generalist (not ML-focused) and listed as "founding" level, implying it would be one of the first engineers - a very different role from joining an established engineering team.

4. **UG legal entity with €500 capital.** For a company that raised $4.47M in February 2026, still operating as a UG rather than converting to a GmbH is unusual and signals incomplete corporate infrastructure. Employment contract terms and employment law protections are technically the same under a UG, but it is a yellow flag.

**Secondary concerns:** No employee reviews on any platform (0 Kununu, no Glassdoor) means culture and management quality cannot be independently assessed. The "comfort with chaos" language in JDs and the founding-stage structure suggest high-intensity, unpredictable work conditions. Munich office address has not been confirmed despite all roles listing Munich as location.

**Recommendation:** Do not pursue. Monitor if YOU(th) reaches Series B (indicating sustained growth and extended runway), converts to GmbH, opens a Staff-level ML engineering role, and discloses compensation in the Munich market range. None of those conditions are currently met.
