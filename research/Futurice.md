# Futurice - Full Research Report

_Researched: 2026-11-16_

> **Critical Disqualifiers**
> 1. **Consulting company.** Futurice is a digital consultancy: engineers are placed on client projects, postings ask for a "true consultant mindset and interest in supporting sales", and the company states that "we usually don't own the software we produce". The German entity is registered under WZ 62.02 (IT consulting) and 70.22 (management consulting) as well as software development.
> 2. **German at C1/C2 was mandatory** on the only recent Munich engineering posting (Principal AI Architect, posted May 2026, now closed).
> 3. **The German business is shrinking fast.** Futurice GmbH revenue fell from €18.3M (2022) to €9.2M (2024), and headcount fell from 111 to 68 in 2024 alone. There are no open engineering roles in Germany (company ATS and LinkedIn, Munich area: 0 results).
>
> The full report follows for completeness.

### Business Model

Digital consultancy: strategy, design, software, data and AI delivered as client projects, plus a managed services arm ("FutuCare", 60+ people, Finland) that runs and maintains client systems. Around it sits the "Futurice Family" of specialist companies co-founded and majority-owned by Futurice (Columbia Road, Thriv, Meltlake, Recordly, Fram Partners, Qlarify, Way Interim). Thriv and Fram Partners are staff-augmentation businesses.

### Software-Centric Assessment

The work is software engineering, and the registered purpose of the German GmbH is software development. The product, however, is billable expertise. The company's own interview-process blog states that "we usually don't own the software we produce". There is no proprietary product line with its own roadmap that an engineer would join. A former Berlin software developer describes the reality as "mostly CRUD apps" (Glassdoor, Feb 2023, former Software Developer, Berlin, 3+ years).

## 6. AI/ML & Tech Stack

## 13. Verdict

**Exclude.**

**Strengths.** Colleagues and culture are praised on both platforms, German reviewers report high work-life balance (Glassdoor 4.6) with compensated overtime and real autonomy, the German entity employs directly and carries no debt, the Munich office is a few minutes from Sendlinger Tor, and the AI work is genuinely applied (GenAI, LLMOps, data platforms) rather than marketing.

**Hard blockers.**

1. **Consulting business model.** Engineers are consultants on client engagements: postings ask for a "true consultant mindset and interest in supporting sales", the register lists IT and management consulting, reviewers describe billable-hour and utilisation targets, and staffing follows individual client contracts (a 2026 Berlin hire ended during probation when the client cut scope). This fails both the software-centric and the not-consulting criteria.
2. **German as a primary requirement.** The one recent Munich engineering posting stated "German skills at a C1/C2 is mandatory", and German clients are large German corporates. This follows from the model rather than one posting.
3. **Salary below bracket.** The best German senior engineering band on Glassdoor is €60K to €87K (5 data points), Senior Software Engineer €57K to €64K (7), levels.fyi Germany median €57.1K (12), all with no equity, against a Germany senior median of about €95K. Pay is the top complaint from Berlin and Munich reviewers.

**Secondary concerns.** The German subsidiary is contracting: revenue €18.3M to €9.2M in two years, headcount 111 to 68 in 2024. Layoffs recur (Germany 2022/2023 and 2024, Finland 2023 and 2026), with no works council or collective agreement in either country. German management ratings are poor (senior management 2.2, CEO approval 17%, business outlook 14%), and Munich has only two Glassdoor reviews to go on. On-call and regulatory exposure depend on whichever client one is assigned to. There are no open engineering roles in Germany.

**Recommendation.** Do not pursue. No further research is warranted: blockers 1 and 2 follow from the business model, and the new financial and review data only reinforce the exclusion. If the consulting exclusion were ever relaxed, the points to verify would be the German-language requirement for the specific role, the salary band (which would need to clear €95K to be competitive), and whether the Munich office has a stable client base after the 2024 reduction.
