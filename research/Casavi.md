**Reading this.** *Nicht durch Eigenkapital gedeckter Fehlbetrag* is the German accounting device for **negative equity**: accumulated losses have exceeded paid-in capital and reserves by €3.02M, and the shortfall is parked on the asset side to balance. In German practice this is normally survivable only because shareholders hold subordinated loans with a *Rangrücktritt* (subordination agreement), which prevents it from constituting insolvency-triggering *Überschuldung* under § 19 InsO. casavi's shareholder base (Digital+ Partners, Bayern Kapital, HTGF) is exactly the kind that provides such instruments, and the company is plainly operating normally - it hired in July 2026 and made an acquisition in September 2025. But the position is unambiguous: **casavi has consumed all of its equity and is running on shareholder support.**

Two further observations: half the balance sheet (€5.2M of €10.4M) is the Managbl.AI participation, a non-cash, non-liquid intangible-like asset whose carrying value depends on that acquisition working out. And receivables of €398K against ~2,000 subscription customers is very low, consistent with prepaid annual SaaS billing - which is the one genuine cash-flow positive in this picture.

Positive trend: the loss narrowed from €5.17M (2023) to €3.05M (2024), a ~41% improvement, which suggests a deliberate and reasonably effective march toward break-even.

## Section 13: Verdict

**Recommendation: Monitor, do not pursue.** casavi is a genuinely likeable company attached to a balance sheet that fails the single criterion of a mature, profitable, stable employer worth committing to long-term.

**Strengths.** This is, culturally, one of the better small employers researched. A Kununu score of **4.6 across 146 reviews with 92% employee recommendation and 82 of 89 employee reviews at four or five stars** is not a fluke of small numbers. Leadership is credited with communicating openly when things go wrong, work-life balance draws no complaints at all across five years of reviews, homeoffice is confirmed by 80% of reviewers, and the highest-rated single factor in the entire profile is **treatment of older colleagues at 4.9** - Toxic-leadership risk is LOW on every pattern checked. The office is at Sandstraße 33 (Maxvorstadt), and the role is remote-eligible on top. The German entity is the real operating company with a development-first registered purpose and permanent direct contracts. The stack is Python-primary on AWS and Kubernetes, the AI investment is real (three shipped products, an AI company bought outright), the interview process is experience-based rather than LeetCode-based, and there is **an open, verified Senior Software Engineer (Python) role right now**.

**Hard blockers:**

1. **Negative equity and effectively zero runway.** The FY2025 balance sheet filed 06.08.2026 shows a *Nicht durch Eigenkapital gedeckter Fehlbetrag* of **€3,024,913** - accumulated losses have consumed all paid-in capital - against **€622,521** of cash. Base-case net burn of ~€2.75M/year gives **roughly 2.7 months of runway on the cash line**. The company is evidently still operating on shareholder loans and prepaid SaaS collections, but its solvency rests on investor willingness rather than on the business. This is the opposite of "mature, profitable, stable".

2. **No priced funding round since 16 December 2021.** Four years and eight months. The September 2025 capital increase (nominal €87,527 → €90,936) coincides exactly with the MANAGBL.AI acquisition, the new Satzung and the new Gesellschafterliste, and is share consideration, not fresh cash. A company burning €3M/year that has not raised in nearly five years is either about to close a round on weak terms or about to be sold - neither is "about to exit" in the favourable sense.

3. **Compensation is €20-40K below the Munich senior bracket with zero equity to close the gap.** The best senior data point available anywhere is **€80,000** (Glassdoor, 2 submissions); the Kununu Softwareentwickler average is **€67,600**; the Engineering Manager average of €86,700 still sits below the Munich *individual contributor* tech median of €109,172. The benefits list in the current posting includes bAV, Kita-Zuschuss, EGYM Wellpass and 60 workation days but **no VSOP, ESOP, options or phantom shares** - unusual for a pre-exit VC-backed company, and it removes the only mechanism that normally makes a below-market base rational at this stage. A Kununu reviewer names the mechanism: *"reine Verhandlungssache, keine Lohngleichheit"*. Even a strong negotiation lands well short. And on the equity point, blocker 1 makes any hypothetical VSOP close to worthless in expectation.

**Secondary concerns.**

- **The role is not the AI/ML platform work being targeted.** The AI requirement resolves to "use LLMs as components of software architecture" plus "be a power user of GitHub Copilot". That is applied product engineering with an LLM call in it, not platform, MLOps, or infrastructure work.
- **Frontend is mandatory, not incidental.** *"You feel equally at home in the backend as well as on the frontend"* - React SPA work is explicitly in scope, with backend merely the "main strength".
- **Engineers specifically report weak product direction.** The one Glassdoor review from a Senior Software Engineer (Jan 2024, 3.0, would not recommend) says *"das Softwareprodukt ist auf einem niedrigen Niveau, was Stress und Missverständnisse mit den Kollegen mit sich bringt. Agile Praktiken werden auf eine sehr merkwürdige Weise umgesetzt."* A Kununu reviewer independently asks management to *"[den Entwicklern] mehr Führung und klare Zielsetzungen geben. Die sinnlose Feature-Massenproduktion aufhören."* Two sources, two platforms, same complaint - and it is the complaint most specific to the engineering function.
- **Onboarding is weak for lateral hires**, per the October 2022 review describing no clear objectives, mockery of questions, and teamlead feedback not reaching management. Old and uncorroborated, but it is the failure mode that would matter most on entry.
- **Constant soft revenue pressure explicitly tied to the financing situation** (Dec 2025): *"seit Jahren soft aber präsent Druck vermittelt, die Zahlen zu erreichen... bei einem Scale Up Unternehmen mit teilw. fremdfinanzierten Mitteln."* Reviewers are feeling blocker 1 from the inside.
- **Glassdoor and Indeed are effectively blind here** (2 reviews and 0 reviews respectively), so the entire favourable culture picture rests on Kununu - where casavi maintains a paid employer profile and actively solicits reviews, quoting its own 4.6 score in the job ad.
- **Candidate handling is inconsistent** - two Munich engineering candidates in 2024 report rejection after two weeks with no feedback and no reply to follow-ups.
- **On-call is unverified** and should be assumed to exist in some form for 2,000-customer "highly available" SaaS.
- **German-language ambiguity** - the engineering posting requires only English, but the company's own Kununu profile says good German is a must for "almost all" positions, and the entire customer base is German-speaking.

**Recommendation.** Do not apply. Two independent hard blockers each disqualify on their own: a balance sheet with negative equity and under three months of cash runway, and a senior compensation ceiling around €80K with no equity in a market whose median is €109K. The culture, the Python/AWS stack and the live senior opening are all genuinely attractive, which makes this a near-miss rather than a bad company - but the criteria call for a mature, profitable employer worth a long-term commitment, and casavi is currently a loss-making scale-up whose continued existence depends on shareholders topping it up.

**What would change this.** Three things, together: (1) a closed Series C or a strategic acquisition by a solvent parent that restores positive equity and puts 18+ months of cash on the balance sheet; (2) an FY2026 filing showing break-even or a materially smaller loss; and (3) a senior band that starts at €100K+, or a meaningful equity grant made credible by (1). If a recruiter approaches directly, it is worth one exploratory call purely to test the salary band and to ask what funding closed in 2026 - but on today's public record there is no reason to initiate.
